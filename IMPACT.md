# Impact — Workspace Cap Matrix

**Repository:** `GlacierEQ/replit-workspace-cap-matrix`  
**Company lens:** Replit

## Near-term
Workspace Cap Matrix now has an executable impact surface for comparing capability gains against failure cost. High-value changes with weak evidence or excessive blast radius are driven toward hardening before promotion.

## Long-term
The mechanism remains specialized for Replit, while its impact vector is comparable across the 53-repository innovation fleet. This enables portfolio-level compounding without homogenizing implementation.

## Failure and recovery
Blast radius, reversibility, and evidence are first-class inputs. Ambitious changes score higher when they are both powerful and recoverable.

## Expansion frontier
Feed benchmark/runtime observations into `src/impact_assurance.py`, then use the score to select the next domain-mechanism enhancement. Optimize capability and proof together.
